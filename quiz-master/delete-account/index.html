Delete your QuizMaster account

QuizMaster · Arovyx Studio

This page explains how to delete your QuizMaster account (app by Arovyx Studio) and the data linked to it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open QuizMaster and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm, then log in once more (enter your password, or pick your Google account).

Your account is deleted straight away, and the app carries on as a new guest.

Playing as a guest? Guests have no cloud account. Your progress is only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Without the app

Email app.development.arovyx@gmail.com from the address of your QuizMaster account (or the Google account you log in with), with the subject QuizMaster account deletion. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, and we delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email or Google sign-in) and profile: display name, avatar, profile photo and email address.
  • Your username, which becomes free for others to take.
  • Your game progress: coins and coin history, level and XP, stats, achievements, Daily Challenge history and unlocks.
  • Your scores. Leaderboards drop you at their next update, within a few hours.
  • Your friends list (you are also removed from your friends' lists), friend requests you sent or received, challenges, and quiz rooms you host.
  • Your progress saved on the phone you delete from.

What is kept

  • Records of Google Play purchases, where needed for accounting, tax and fraud prevention.
  • Analytics and crash data, which isn't linked to your name or email, for up to 14 months (crash reports 90 days).

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus or Pro subscription. Cancel it in Google Play subscriptions.
