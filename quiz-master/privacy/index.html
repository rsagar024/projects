Privacy Policy

QuizMaster · Arovyx Studio · Effective 20 October 2026

This policy explains what the QuizMaster app (package dev.arovyx.games.quizmaster, "the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. QuizMaster is made by Arovyx Studio ("we"). Questions: app.development.arovyx@gmail.com.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. As a guest, your progress (coins, scores, achievements, settings) is stored only on your phone. The app still creates an anonymous Firebase sign-in for your install, and the analytics, crash reporting, notifications and ads described below still apply.

Who can see your data

Your profile is visible to other QuizMaster players so they can find you, add you as a friend and see you on leaderboards. Your profile includes your display name, username, avatar, profile photo, email address, level and XP, quiz stats, achievements and plan badge. Your scores are also visible on leaderboards. If you don't want other players to see your email address, log in with a Google or Email account whose address you are comfortable sharing, or keep playing as a guest.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who processes your data

Your data is stored and processed by Google services that act for us: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Remote Config, Google Play Billing and Google Play In-App Updates. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

App updates may be delivered through Shorebird, which receives only the app version and a device check for updates, never your player data.

New daily quiz questions are written with an AI service (NVIDIA NIM) on our servers. It receives only quiz topics, never any player data.

Ads are served by these partners, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • InMobi: inmobi.com/privacy-policy
  • Start.io: start.io/policy/privacy-policy-site
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy

Some of this processing may happen outside India.

Ads and consent

Free players see ads. Ad partners may receive your advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show, measure and personalise ads and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice in Settings → About → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Players with a Plus or Pro plan see no ads.

Permissions

  • Camera: only while you scan a friend's QR code. The picture never leaves your phone.
  • Notifications: daily quiz reminders and weekly summaries. You can turn them off.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and advertising ID: to sync your progress and show ads.

How long we keep data

  • Account, progress and social data: until you delete your account.
  • Records of Google Play purchases: kept after account deletion where needed for accounting, tax and fraud prevention.
  • Analytics data: kept for 14 months; crash reports for 90 days.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, username, progress, scores, friends, friend requests, challenges and the quiz rooms you host. Leaderboards drop you within a few hours. If you can't use the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications on or off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → About → Ad privacy settings.
  • Log out at any time; your progress returns when you log in again.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by email.

Children

QuizMaster is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and database rules stop players from changing anyone else's data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
