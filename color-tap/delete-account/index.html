Delete Your Account

Color Tap — Fast Reflex Game

Last updated: October 15, 2026

You can permanently delete your Color Tap account, and the data linked to it, at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data. Color Tap is developed by Arovyx Studio.

Summary: Delete your account instantly from Profile → Delete Account in the App, or email us and we will delete it within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and does not cancel an active subscription.

1. Delete in the App (instant)

  1. Open Color Tap and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap your profile avatar on the Home screen to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping DELETE.

Your account and cloud data are removed straight away. You need an internet connection, and you may be asked to sign in again first.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the App installed or cannot sign in, email us from the address registered to your account:

  • To: app.development.arovyx@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my Color Tap account
  • Include: Your registered email and your username, if you claimed one

Email a Deletion Request

We may ask you to confirm that you own the account. Verified requests are completed within 30 days, and we will email you when it is done.

3. What Gets Deleted

DataWhat happens
Sign-in account (email, Google link or guest ID)Deleted
Profile (display name, avatar, country, stats, achievements)Deleted
Username reservationDeleted, and the name becomes free for others to claim
Game scores and historyDeleted
Leaderboard entries (weekly and daily bests)Deleted, and you are removed from the Global, Country and Friends boards

4. What May Be Kept

  • Support messages: Kept for up to 90 days after they are resolved, then deleted. Ask in your email if you want them removed sooner.
  • Analytics & crash data: Not linked to your name or email. It expires under Firebase's standard retention: up to 14 months for analytics and 90 days for crash reports.
  • Purchase records: Kept by Google Play or Apple under their own policies and legal obligations.
  • Legal obligations: Any data we must keep by law, such as for fraud prevention or legal claims, is kept only as long as required.
Before you delete
  • Deletion is permanent. Your scores and progress cannot be restored.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings (Subscriptions).

5. Guest Players

If you never registered, your game data is stored only on your device. To remove it, uninstall the App or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Color Tap → Storage → Clear data). If you have questions about a guest account, email us.

For more about how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
