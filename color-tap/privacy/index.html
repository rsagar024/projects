Privacy Policy

Color Tap — Fast Reflex Game

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arovyx Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use the Color Tap mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary: We collect only the data needed to run the game: your scores, gameplay analytics and, if you register, your account details. We do not sell your personal data. Registered players' leaderboard scores are visible to other players by design. Guest data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: When the App first opens, you are signed in as an anonymous guest, which has a random ID and no personal details. If you register with Google or with an email and password, we collect your email address and display name.
  • Profile data: Your chosen avatar, display name, unique username (claimed once and permanent) and country.
  • Gameplay data: Scores, difficulty, correct and wrong answers, best combo, game duration, timestamps, achievements and lifetime statistics.
  • Support requests: When you use Contact Us, we collect the category, subject and message, along with your email, display name and username so we can reply.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data: App opens, screen views, game start and end events and feature interactions, through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports: Stack traces, device state and app version, through Firebase Crashlytics, so we can diagnose and fix bugs.
  • Advertising ID: Your device's advertising identifier (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS where permitted). Our ad partners collect it to personalise and measure ads.
  • Device information: OS version, device model, app version, language and timezone.
  • Network information: IP address and connection status, used for network requests and offline sync.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your basic profile (name and email) with us.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store: Your subscription status for the Plus and Pro tiers. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Ad networks (AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, ironSource, InMobi, Start.io): These partners may collect data as described in their own privacy policies.

3. Data Storage

3.1 Local Storage

Game scores, settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, reminder) and profile preferences are stored on your device using SharedPreferences. This data stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

3.2 Cloud Storage

When you register an account, your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, including:

  • Your user profile (display name, email, avatar, country, statistics, achievements, subscription tier)
  • Game score records (score, difficulty, combo, duration, timestamp, country)
  • Leaderboard entries (weekly bests per difficulty and country, and daily bests)
  • Your username reservation (a unique, permanent username)
  • Your support tickets

3.3 Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is kept while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Deleting your account in the App immediately removes your profile, username, scores and leaderboard entries. Requests sent by email are completed within 30 days. See Delete Account.
  • Support tickets: Kept for up to 90 days after they are resolved.
  • Analytics data: Kept under Firebase's default retention (up to 14 months for event data).
  • Crash reports: Kept for 90 days.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Leaderboard visibility: Registered players' display name, username, avatar, score, difficulty and country are visible to other registered players on the Global, Country and Friends leaderboards. Guests never appear on them.
  • Service providers: Google (Firebase, Play Billing, Sign-In), Apple (App Store, StoreKit) and our ad network partners process data on our behalf, under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if a law, regulation or legal process requires it.
  • Business transfers: If there is a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, user data may be transferred to the new owner.

5. Advertising

The App shows third-party ads through a unified ad waterfall that includes AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, ironSource, InMobi and Start.io. These partners may use your advertising ID and device information to show personalised ads.

  • Banner ads: Shown at the bottom of some screens.
  • Interstitial ads: Full-screen ads shown between games, limited by a cooldown.
  • Rewarded ads: Optional ads you can watch to continue after a game over, get a 2× score boost or claim a daily bonus. They have a daily limit.

If you subscribe to Color Tap Plus or Color Tap Pro, all banner and interstitial ads are removed. Rewarded ads stay available because they are optional and give in-game benefits.

6. App Permissions

  • Internet & network state: To sync scores, load ads and show the offline indicator.
  • Advertising ID: For ads, as described above.
  • Billing: For in-app subscriptions.
  • Notifications (optional): For the daily reminder. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your display name, avatar and country in your in-app profile.
  • Deletion: Delete your account from the Profile screen, or follow the steps on our Delete Account page.
  • Portability: Ask for your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Reset your advertising ID, or use "Delete advertising ID" (Android) or "Allow Apps to Request to Track" (iOS) in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent: Stop using the App and delete your account at any time.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • All data is sent over TLS encryption.
  • Firestore security rules restrict access to user data: players can write only their own records, and only registered players can read leaderboards.
  • Firebase Authentication stores credentials using industry-standard hashing. We never see your password.
  • Score validation rules stop impossible scores from being uploaded.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

10. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in India and the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards.

11. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to external websites, such as the Play Store rating page or social sharing. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will tell you about significant changes through an in-app notice, or by updating the "Last updated" date above. If you keep using the App after a change, you accept the revised policy.
