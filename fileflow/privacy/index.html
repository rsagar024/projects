Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 26 November 2026

FileFlow lets you store, browse and sync your files using your own Telegram account as the storage. This policy explains what information the FileFlow app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

FileFlow has no servers of its own and no FileFlow account. Your files, phone number, login code and password go directly between your device and Telegram — Arovyx Studio never receives them. The only data that reaches us (through Google Firebase) is pseudonymous app usage statistics and crash reports.

1. Who we are

FileFlow (the “app”, Android package dev.arovyx.apps.fileflow) is developed and published by Arovyx Studio (“we”, “us”). For anything about this policy or your data, write to app.development.arovyx@gmail.com.

FileFlow is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Telegram.

2. How FileFlow works

  • You sign in with your Telegram account — by phone number and login code (plus your two-step verification password if you have one), or by scanning a QR code with Telegram on another device. Sign-in uses Telegram’s official client library (TDLib) running on your device.
  • FileFlow creates a private storage group in your Telegram account and uploads the files you choose to it. Telegram stores them; FileFlow organises them into folders on your device.
  • There is no FileFlow server, no FileFlow user account and no FileFlow database in the cloud. We cannot see, open, restore or delete your files or your Telegram account.

3. Data handled through Telegram

When you use FileFlow, the following is exchanged directly between your device and Telegram, and is governed by Telegram’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service:

  • Sign-in details: your phone number, the login code, your two-step verification password (if set), and the session Telegram creates for this device.
  • Your profile: name, username and profile photo, shown on the You tab. If you edit your name or photo in FileFlow, the change is sent to Telegram.
  • Your files: the files you upload, and a small preview image (up to 320 px) made on your device for images, videos, audio covers and PDFs — never for encrypted files.
  • File details (metadata): each upload carries a caption with the file name, folder, size, type, a content hash (used to detect duplicates), upload time, a favourite flag and, for encrypted files, encryption parameters. A pinned message in the storage group keeps your empty folders and, if you use encryption, your passphrase-protected key.
  • Active sessions: the Devices screen reads your Telegram sessions from Telegram so you can end them.

Arovyx Studio does not receive a copy of any of this.

Videos and audio are streamed to the built-in player through a local server that only listens on your device (127.0.0.1) and cannot be reached from the network. Encrypted files are never streamed this way.

FileFlow only downloads file contents you ask for (opening, previewing, playing, downloading or sharing a file). Starting the app only syncs file details.

5. Optional encryption

You can turn on client-side encryption. Files are then encrypted on your device before upload, so Telegram only stores encrypted data. Your key is protected by a passphrase you choose; the protected key is stored in your Telegram storage group so your other devices can use it, and the unlocked key is kept in your device’s secure storage.

We never see your passphrase and cannot recover it. If you forget it, files you encrypted cannot be opened by anyone.

6. Analytics & crash reports

FileFlow uses Google Firebase services, operated by Google LLC:

  • Firebase Analytics collects automatic, pseudonymous usage events (for example first open, session start, app updates and screens viewed), together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, free memory and storage, and an installation identifier.

These help us understand how the app is used and fix bugs. They do not include your files, file names, folders, phone number, Telegram messages or login details. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

FileFlow shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers or ad networks.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to talk to Telegram.
  • Notifications: to show upload and download progress, the “uploaded” confirmation and media playback controls.
  • Foreground service (data sync, media playback) and wake lock: to keep transfers running and music playing while the app is in the background, and keep the screen on while a video plays.
  • Files you pick or share: FileFlow only reads the files you choose in the system picker or share to it from another app. It does not scan your storage.

8. Sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Besides Telegram (which you use as your storage) and Google Firebase (analytics and crash reports, as above), FileFlow sends your data to no one. We may disclose information we hold only if required by law.

9. Retention & deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you clear the cache, log out or uninstall FileFlow.
  • Your files stay in your Telegram account until you delete them (in FileFlow or in Telegram) or delete the storage group.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for the periods in section 6 and can be deleted on request.

See Delete your account and data for step-by-step instructions.

10. Security

Communication with Telegram is encrypted by Telegram’s MTProto protocol. Keys and settings are kept in the platform’s secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain). Optional client-side encryption adds protection so that even Telegram stores only encrypted data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because your files and account data live in your own Telegram account and on your device, you can manage most of it directly. For analytics and crash data, email us at app.development.arovyx@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

12. Children

FileFlow is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and Telegram itself requires users to meet its minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used FileFlow, contact us and we will request deletion of any related analytics data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on this page before they take effect.
