Delete your account and data

App: FileFlow by Arovyx Studio · Last updated: 26 November 2026

FileFlow has no account of its own — you sign in with your Telegram account, and your files are stored in your Telegram account. There is nothing on a FileFlow server to delete. This page shows how to remove everything FileFlow created.

What FileFlow keeps, and where

• On your device: sign-in session, file index, cached files, previews, settings and encryption key.
• In your Telegram account: the private FileFlow storage group with your uploaded files.
• With Google Firebase: pseudonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

  1. Log out of FileFlow on each device

    Open FileFlow → You tab → Log out. This ends FileFlow’s Telegram session and deletes the file index, cached and downloaded files, previews, transfer queue, settings and encryption key from that device. Then uninstall the app if you like.

    To sign out other devices at once: You tab → Devices → Log out all other devices (or end sessions in Telegram → Settings → Devices).

  2. Delete your stored files

    Your files remain in your Telegram account until you remove them. To delete all of them, open Telegram, find the private FileFlow storage group, then open its info → Delete and leave group (choose to delete it for everyone). To delete only some files, delete them inside FileFlow before logging out.

    This is permanent. Download anything you want to keep first.

  3. Ask us to delete analytics and crash data

    Email app.development.arovyx@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – FileFlow”. Since this data isn’t linked to your name or phone number, tell us your device model and the approximate dates you used the app so we can find it. We will request deletion from Google Firebase within 30 days and confirm by email.

    If you don’t ask, this data is deleted automatically: analytics after at most 14 months, crash reports after 90 days.

  4. Optional: delete your Telegram account

    Deleting your Telegram account is separate from FileFlow and is done by Telegram. It removes all your Telegram messages, contacts and the FileFlow storage group. Go to my.telegram.org/delete, or in Telegram: Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete my account / If away for.
